# Saarthi — Closed Beta Privacy Notice & Data Policy

**Effective Date**: August 20, 2026  
**Application Version**: `v1.0.0-beta.1`  

---

## 100% Local AI & Privacy Principles

1. **Zero External AI Transmissions**:
   - All resume text, job descriptions, cover letters, STAR interview responses, and Career Copilot chat prompts are processed **100% locally** on your computer via local Ollama and NVIDIA Nemotron models.
   - Zero prompt text is transmitted to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or any external cloud AI provider.
2. **Local Storage & Data Ownership**:
   - Your resumes, job application records, and career database are stored locally in SQLite (`data/database/saarthi.db`).
   - You retain full control and ownership of all local career documents.
3. **Anonymous Operational Telemetry**:
   - Beta operational logs record strictly anonymous UI navigation event names (e.g. `PRICING_VIEWED`, `UPGRADE_PROMPT_SHOWN`).
   - **Zero personal career data, prompts, or AI outputs are ever collected or transmitted.**
