# Saarthi — Closed Beta Tester Guide

**Welcome to the Saarthi Closed Beta!**  
Thank you for helping us test Saarthi — the local-first AI Career Operating System powered by NVIDIA Nemotron models.

---

## Core Features to Test

1. **Multi-Experience Shells**: Navigate to **Settings** -> **Theme & Experience** and switch between **SIGNATURE**, **FORGE**, **PULSE**, and **AURORA**.
2. **ATS Resume Analysis**: Upload a Markdown/Text resume, paste a Job Description, and view score breakdown, keyword matches, and missing skill suggestions.
3. **Factually Grounded Resume Tailoring**: Tailor resume bullet points for specific job applications without synthetic exaggeration.
4. **Cover Letter Synthesis**: Generate targeted cover letters customized to specific companies and roles.
5. **STAR Interview Prep**: Practice behavioral and technical interview questions with STAR scoring rubrics.
6. **Career Kanban Tracker**: Save job applications across **Saved**, **Applied**, **Interview**, and **Offer** pipeline stages.
7. **Document Export & Watermarking**: Export documents to Markdown/PDF format. Verify watermarking behavior on Free tier vs clean exports on Student/Personal/Power tiers.
8. **Offline Local Privacy Test**: Disconnect your internet connection after setup and verify that all career AI features continue operating locally.

---

## Feedback & Problem Reporting

If you encounter any unexpected behavior or performance issues:
- Note your machine specs (CPU, System RAM, GPU, VRAM).
- Copy the operational log snippet from **Settings** -> **AI Status**.
- Submit feedback via the **Beta Feedback** button in the top navigation header.
