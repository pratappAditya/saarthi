# Phase AA Controlled Pricing Experiment Execution Report

## Overview
Execution audit of Saarthi's controlled real-world pricing experiment evaluating Student ₹149/month Control vs Student ₹100/month Treatment.

---

## Locked Commercial Pricing Matrix (Rule AA.3)

### 1. India Region (INR)
- **FREE**: ₹0 / month (Hidden Saarthi watermark on exported documents)
- **STUDENT (Control)**: ₹149 / month | ₹1,299 / year
- **STUDENT (Treatment)**: ₹100 / month | ₹899 / year
- **PERSONAL**: ₹399 / month | ₹3,499 / year
- **POWER**: ₹799 / month | ₹6,999 / year

### 2. International Region (USD)
- **FREE**: $0 / month (Hidden Saarthi watermark on exported documents)
- **STUDENT**: $1.50 / month | $12.99 / year
- **PERSONAL**: $5 / month | $44.99 / year
- **POWER**: $10 / month | $89.99 / year

---

## Primary Metric: Revenue Per Eligible Exposed User ($\text{RPEU}$)
- **Formula**:
  $$\text{RPEU} = \frac{\text{Realized Student Subscription Revenue}}{\text{Eligible Exposed Users in Variant}}$$
- **Decision Principle**: Lowering the Student price to ₹100 / month can ONLY be declared superior if $\text{RPEU}$ is higher or acceptably non-inferior without harming cohort retention or candidate application rates.
