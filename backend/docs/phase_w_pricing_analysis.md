## Pricing Decision
- **Action**: **KEEP CURRENT PRICING UNCHANGED**.
- **Rationale**: 0.0% refund rate and 0.0% chargeback rate demonstrate zero price resistance for Student ₹149/mo. Altering pricing before public beta cohort volume expands would add unnecessary risk.
